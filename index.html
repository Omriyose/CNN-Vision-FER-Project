Instructions

  1. Add a face photo: drag it in, tap the drop zone, or use your phone camera.
  2. Check the preview to make sure the face is clear.
  3. Press Classify.
  4. Read the emotion and the confidence bars.
  5. Optionally save the image.
Tip: Close-up, front-facing, well-lit faces work best.

How it works

Your photo goes through three steps on the server before you see a result.

Input

Your photo

Any size, colour or format.

Step 1

Resize to 48×48 greyscale

Same format as the training images.

Step 2

Convolutional network

Three conv blocks pick out edges, then features like eyes and mouth.

Step 3

7 probabilities

The highest one is the answer, if it's above 60%.

Data

FER2013: about 36,000 small 48×48 greyscale faces, each labelled with one of 7 emotions.

angrydisgustfear happyneutralsadsurprise

Model

A convolutional neural network with three blocks of two convolution layers, batch normalization, pooling and dropout.

Trained in Google Colab and served with FastAPI.

Honest limits

Roughly 61% accuracy on the test set. Fear and surprise get confused easily.

If confidence is under 60%, the app says "unknown" instead of guessing.
